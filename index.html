How It Works

🔎
Seerr — Find & Request
This is where you start. Search for any movie or TV show you want to watch, then hit Request. It'll automatically download in the background — usually takes a few minutes to a couple hours depending on the size. You'll see the status change from "Requested" to "Available" when it's ready.
Open
🎬
Jellyfin — Watch
This is where you watch everything. Once a movie or show is downloaded, it shows up here automatically. You need an account to watch — I'll create one for you. Sign in with the same login on Seerr too, so you don't need separate passwords.
Open
📷
Immich — Photo Backup
Private photo and video storage, like Google Photos but hosted by me. Other users can't see your photos. As the server owner, I have admin access to everything stored on it. You get a separate login for this one. The phone app can automatically back up your camera roll.

Links Not Loading?

If Seerr, Jellyfin, or Immich just spins or says the site can't be reached, it's almost always Tailscale. Go through these in order:

  1. Is Tailscale on? Open the Tailscale app and make sure it says Connected (switch it on if it's off).
  2. Did you actually accept the invite? Click the invite link again. The first time, it may only ask you to log in or sign up. Keep going until you see the Accept invite button (see the picture in step 2), and click it.
  3. Right account in the app? In the Tailscale app, tap your account name and make sure you're signed in with the same account you used for the invite. If it lists more than one network, pick the one you were invited to.
  4. Still stuck? Text me a screenshot of the Tailscale app and I'll sort it out.

On Your Phone

Everything works in your phone's browser, but if you'd rather have apps, here are your options. Keep Tailscale connected either way.

  • Watch and request in one app (easiest): install Streamyfin (iPhone and Android). Enter http://100.86.174.40:8096 as the server and sign in with your Jellyfin login. Then open Streamyfin's settings, turn on Seerr, and enter http://100.86.174.40:5055. You can now search, request, and watch without switching apps.
  • Just watching: the official Jellyfin app works too. Use http://100.86.174.40:8096 as the server address.
  • Seerr as an app icon: open http://100.86.174.40:5055 in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android). Tap Share → Add to Home Screen (in Chrome, it's the ⋮ menu). It then opens like an app. There's nothing to install.
  • Photos: install the Immich app and use http://100.86.174.40:2283 as the server address. It can back up your camera roll automatically.
